What is the Tech Radar?

The Glanbia Tech Radar is a list of technologies, complemented by an assessment result, called ring assignment. We use four rings with the following semantics:

  • ADOPT — Technologies we have high confidence in to serve our purpose, also in large scale. Technologies with a usage culture in our Glanbia production environment, low risk and recommended to be widely used.
  • TRIAL — Technologies that we have seen work with success in project work to solve a real problem; first serious usage experience that confirm benefits and can uncover limitations. TRIAL technologies are slightly more risky.
  • ASSESS — Technologies that are promising and have clear potential value-add for us; technologies worth investing some research and prototyping efforts in to see if it has impact. ASSESS technologies have higher risks; they are often brand new and highly unproven in our organisation.
  • HOLD — Technologies not recommended to be used for new projects. Technologies that we think are not (yet) worth to (further) invest in. HOLD technologies should not be used for new projects, but usually can be continued for existing projects.

How to Use This Radar

The radar is divided into four quadrants representing different technology categories. Each quadrant contains technologies placed in rings based on our assessment:

  • Hover over any technology to see its full name and details
  • Click on technologies with links to learn more about them
  • Triangle pointing up ▲ indicates the technology has moved to an inner ring (more recommended)
  • Triangle pointing down ▼ indicates the technology has moved to an outer ring (less recommended)
  • Circle ● indicates no movement since the last update

This radar is updated regularly to reflect our evolving technology landscape. The current version is from January 2026.
